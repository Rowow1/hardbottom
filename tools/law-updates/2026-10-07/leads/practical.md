# Practical rules review: what a Florida spearfisher gets cited for, beyond area closures

Track of the 7 Oct 2026 legal review. Scope: licences, diver-down, gear, size/bag/season for the species spearfishers take most, other resources, and tips. "Registry" means /home/claude/review/registry-index.tsv (278 rows) and data/law.json; "species panel" means data/species.json; "regs panel" means data/regs.json.

How to read the status marks. PRIMARY: read this session on flsenate.gov, leg.state.fl.us, flrules.org or ecfr.gov. Every read went through WebFetch, which summarises, so a quote must be checked word for word in a browser before it goes into law.json. LII: rule text read on law.cornell.edu because flrules serves only a .doc; dates confirmed on flrules. LEAD: news, FWC web pages or other secondary material, to be verified before use.

## Missing or wrong in the registry or species panel (ranked)

1. **State descending-device rule is missing.** Fla. Admin. Code r. 68B-14.005(3) (LII; rule eff. 1 Apr 2023 per flrules ch. 68B-14 index) says that persons on a vessel harvesting or attempting to harvest the listed reef fish "in or from Florida waters" "must be able to access a venting tool or a descending device that is rigged and ready to use." The text has no spearfishing exemption. The registry has only the federal South Atlantic rule (`cfr-622-188-a4`). Conservative reading, matching the project's reading of § 622.188: a spearfishing boat needs one aboard in state waters on both coasts. URL: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=68B-14.005. Add it to `fac-68b-14-005` or as a new row, and to the regs panel.
2. **Landing in whole condition is missing entirely.** State: r. 68B-14.006(4) (LII; rule eff. 1 Apr 2020 per flrules) covers "all fish harvested from Florida or adjacent federal Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) waters"; deheaded, filleted, skinned or scaled fish may not be possessed in state waters; "Mere evisceration or "gutting" of fish, or mere removal of gills from fish, before landing is not prohibited." Federal: 50 C.F.R. § 622.186 (South Atlantic snapper-grouper "must be maintained with head and fins intact"; may be "eviscerated, gilled, and scaled"; Bahamas skin-on fillet exception) and § 622.10 (Gulf and Caribbean finfish; bait exemption; consumption aboard limited to 1.5 lb of parts per person, cooked aboard). PRIMARY for both CFR sections (eCFR, up to date 28 and 29 Sep 2026). Other 68B chapters have their own "landed in whole condition" rules (rr. 68B-33.003, 68B-41.003, 68B-43.003, 68B-44.003, 68B-47.003, 68B-57.003 by title in search results; not read).
3. **National park weapon rules for spearguns are missing.** 36 C.F.R. § 1.4 defines "Weapon" to include "speargun" and "hand-thrown spear", and defines an unloaded speargun as one whose loading or discharging mechanism "is not charged or drawn". 36 C.F.R. § 2.4(c): "Carrying or possessing a loaded weapon in a motor vehicle, vessel or other mode of transportation is prohibited." PRIMARY (eCFR, up to date 6 Oct 2026; § 2.4 last amended 18 Sep 2018). This applies in Biscayne NP, Dry Tortugas NP, Everglades NP, Canaveral NS and Gulf Islands NS waters. The registry carries § 2.3(e) and § 7.27 but not § 1.4 or § 2.4. Practical effect: a cocked gun on deck in Biscayne is a federal violation even where spearing is lawful. URLs: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1.4, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-2.4.
4. **Scamp and yellowmouth grouper, South Atlantic federal waters: species panel is out of date.** Snapper-Grouper Amendment 55 (91 FR 33661, 4 Jun 2026, effective 6 Jul 2026) set a 1-fish scamp/yellowmouth combined limit inside the 3-fish grouper aggregate. eCFR § 622.187(b)(2) (up to date 6 Oct 2026): "No more than one fish may be a scamp or a yellowmouth grouper, combined." species.json `scamp` and `yellowmouth-grouper` eez_sa say only "Within 3 grouper/tilefish aggregate". FWC's grouper page (LEAD) still shows Atlantic state waters at up to 3, so state and federal now differ. Source for the date: NOAA bulletin https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/bulletin/noaa-fisheries-announces-changes-management-scamp-and-yellowmouth-grouper-south (LEAD for date, CFR is PRIMARY).
5. **Single-species cap inside the South Atlantic 20-fish aggregate is missing, and one note is wrong.** § 622.187(b)(8): "Within the 20-fish bag limit, no more than 10 fish can be of any one of these single snapper-grouper species." species.json `gray-triggerfish` eez_sa says FWC's "up to 10 per person" has "no such figure found in 622.187". The figure is there. The same cap applies to porgies (jolthead, knobbed, whitebone, saucereye), spadefish, white grunt, margate, bar jack, almaco jack and the other species in the aggregate; their eez_sa rows should say "up to 10 of one species within the 20".
6. **Spiny lobster rules a diver is cited for are missing.** The panel says season and bag "not verified here". From LII copies, dates per flrules ch. 68B-24 index: r. 68B-24.003 (eff. 9 Jul 2002) carapace must exceed 3 in, the diver must carry "a measuring device capable of being used to perform the carapace measurement" and measure "in the water", and lobster stays in whole condition; r. 68B-24.004 (eff. 1 Mar 2005) 6 per person per day, sport-season limits 12 (6 in Monroe and Biscayne NP); r. 68B-24.005 (eff. 1 Nov 2018) regular season 6 Aug to 31 Mar, sport season the last Wednesday and Thursday of July; r. 68B-24.006(11), (12) (eff. 1 May 2017) no bleach or liquid-ejecting container while diving with lobster, no lobster from artificial habitat; r. 68B-24.007 (eff. 1 May 2017) no egg-bearing lobster. The lobster permit is § 379.354(8)(d), PRIMARY: "An annual spiny lobster permit for a resident or nonresident to take or possess any spiny lobster". Many spearfishers carry a lobster loop on the same dive, so this belongs in the regs panel even though the spear is banned.
7. **Licence carry duty is missing.** § 379.354(3), PRIMARY: the licence "must be in the personal possession of the person to whom it is issued while the person is taking", and failing to produce it at an officer's request "commits a violation of the law." The registry has the equivalent for the State Reef Fish Survey (r. 68B-14.009(2)) but not for the licence itself. https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/379.354
8. **Diver-down card is incomplete; one size is misattributed.** § 327.331 (PRIMARY; last amended ch. 2016-171, effective 1 Jul 2016; no later amendment) also requires: vessels inside 100 ft / 300 ft slow to headway speed (6); the device "may not be displayed once all divers are aboard or ashore" and no vessel may run with it up unless a diver is in the water (7); vessel display from the highest point or an unobstructed location (8); no display that creates a hazard in a river, inlet or channel (3); a flag stiffener (1)(c)4. Penalty: noncriminal infraction, $100, § 327.73(1)(u) (PRIMARY; § 327.73 last amended ch. 2026-36). law.json `fs-327-331` `full` says the flag must be 12 x 12 in; the statute sizes the symbol, "at least 12 inches by 12 inches in dimension when displayed from the water" and 20 x 24 in from a vessel.
9. **Pending gag and black grouper rule, Atlantic state waters including Monroe.** FWC proposed rule to r. 68B-14.0036, FAR Vol. 52 No. 193, 5 Oct 2026, notice ID 31448290, "federal consistency action" to "modify recreational limits for gag and black grouper in Atlantic state waters, including Monroe County"; comments 5 to 26 Oct 2026. PRIMARY for the notice (https://www.flrules.org/gateway/View_notice.asp?id=31448290); the numbers were not readable (.doc). Add to the currency watch.
10. **Two reef fish rules amended 1 Apr 2026 are still marked secondary.** flrules shows r. 68B-14.0035 and r. 68B-14.0036 amended effective 1 Apr 2026 (final notices 30630483, 30630580). The only change identified is Gulf lane snapper (10 in TL, 20 fish), which species.json already has. Re-read both rules and move `fac-68b-14-0035` and `fac-68b-14-0036` to verbatim or record what else changed.
11. **Charter-diver licence exemption: wording now confirmed, with a gap for freedivers.** § 379.354(7)(f), PRIMARY: "the individual scuba divers engaging in taking or attempting to take saltwater products" from a vessel whose operator holds the vessel licence need no individual licence or permits. law.json marks this "secondary; confirm wording". It says "scuba divers"; a freediver on a dive charter is not clearly covered. Conservative advice: freedivers carry their own licence.
12. **Licence exemptions not re-read are now read.** § 379.353(2) has letters (a) to (q), last amended ch. 2024-251 (PRIMARY). The 65-and-over exemption is (2)(n) and requires carrying a no-cost licence from the tax collector. (2)(l) covers fishing from a licensed pier, not diving. Update the law.json note that says these "were not re-read".
13. **r. 68B-20.003(3) and (6) are not rows.** (3) puts spearers under the same bag, size and season limits as everyone else; (6) makes it unlawful to "transport or possess any fish" taken in violation of the chapter (LII; rule eff. 1 Aug 2014). Low priority; useful as the basis for possession-on-land charges.
14. **Jetty exception is in the registry but not the regs panel.** r. 68B-20.003(2)(d) allows spearfishing "along the last 500 yards of any jetty that extends more than 1,500 yards from the shoreline" (law.json has it verbatim). The regs panel buffer card omits it. Omitting it draws more water closed, which fits the project's posture, but the card should say the exception was deliberately not drawn.
15. **No spearfishing licence or endorsement exists.** FWC's licence page and § 379.354(8) list snook, lobster and other permits but no spearfishing permit. Record as a confirmed negative (LEAD until the whole of subsection (8) is read).
16. **HMS Angling permit, § 635.4(c).** PRIMARY: "The owner of any vessel used to fish recreationally for Atlantic HMS" needs one; sharks also need a shark endorsement. The registry covers the operative spear rule (§ 635.21(h)), so this is a supporting row only.

## Tips and suggestions for the author

1. **Carry three cards, on your person, not in the truck:** licence (§ 379.354(3)), State Reef Fish Survey proof when any of the 13 species could come aboard (r. 68B-14.009(2)), and the lobster permit if a loop is on your belt (§ 379.354(8)(d)). The boater ID applies if born on or after 1 Jan 1988 and running 10 hp or more (§ 327.395(1), (2), PRIMARY, last amended ch. 2025-197).
2. **Keep a rigged descending device aboard on every reef trip,** state and federal, both coasts: r. 68B-14.005(3); § 622.188(a)(4). It costs little and closes off an equipment citation that the text does not excuse for spearfishers.
3. **Gut and gill at the boat; do not fillet, skin or scale until ashore.** r. 68B-14.006(4); § 622.186 (scaling is allowed federally but not under the state rule, so do not scale either).
4. **Measure before you pull the trigger, and know which length.** Hogfish, amberjacks, triggerfish and cobia are fork length; snapper, grouper, sheepshead and flounder are total length (species pages; § 622.2 as the panel already notes). Carry a lobster gauge and measure in the water (r. 68B-24.003).
5. **Flag:** a float flag with a symbol of at least 12 x 12 in, a boat flag at least 20 x 24 in with a stiffener, flown from the highest point; stay within 300 ft (100 ft in an inlet, river or channel); strike the boat flag once everyone is aboard and before running (§ 327.331(1), (4), (5), (7), (8)). The fine is $100 (§ 327.73(1)(u)). A boat on plane near a flag was one of the counts in the July 2025 St. Lucie Inlet case (LEAD).
6. **Load in the water, past the surf.** Juno Beach § 16-4 bans a loaded gun on the beach and pier; Lauderdale-By-The-Sea § 5-4 bans loading anywhere in town; Town of Palm Beach § 74-161 requires guns uncocked on its beaches (all in the registry). In national park waters a loaded speargun aboard is itself a violation (36 C.F.R. §§ 1.4, 2.4(c)).
7. **Transit through the Keys with the gear broken down.** Key Largo and Looe Key management areas allow spear gear only "while passing through without interruption" (§ 922.164(b)(1)(iv)); SPAs and Ecological Reserves allow gear aboard only if "not available for immediate use" (§ 922.164(d)(1)(iii)); in the Upper Keys, a spear in your hand in the water is prima facie evidence (§ 379.2425(2)(b)); state park waters require gear unloaded and stored, passing nonstop (r. 68B-20.003(2)(e)). Practical standard that satisfies all four: shafts out of the guns, bands off, guns below or bagged.
8. **Biscayne:** with lobster aboard, nobody goes in the water inside the Biscayne Bay-Card Sound Spiny Lobster Sanctuary (r. 68B-11.004, in the registry); park minimum sizes and the 10-fish aggregate apply (r. 68B-7.003, 68B-7.004).
9. **Jetties:** FWC does write "spearfishing within 100 feet of a jetty" (South Pointe, Miami Beach, LEAD). Measure from the rocks that are dry at low tide, the conservative reading of "unsubmerged portion" (r. 68B-20.003(2)(d)).
10. **Never shoot snook, tarpon, goliath, permit or pompano, even when they swim into range.** They are the species in every enforcement story found (WPTV 9 Jul 2025; CBS12 11 Mar 2025; Miami Beach jetty case), and all are on r. 68B-20.005.
11. **Under 26 ft and on plane, wear the kill-switch lanyard** (46 U.S.C. § 4312(b), via LII; "operating on plane or above displacement speed"). With a diver down and the boat as pickup, it matters.
12. **Vehicle transport:** no Florida statute names spearguns. § 790.001(20) defines "weapon" with a catch-all "or other deadly weapon" but does not list spearguns (PRIMARY, last amended ch. 2023-18). No case law was found. Keep guns unloaded and bagged in the vehicle; treat this as unsettled rather than safe.

## Working notes (appended per item during the session)

### Item 1: Licences (checked 7 Oct 2026)
- Saltwater licence, § 379.354(1): PRIMARY, flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/379.354. History ends s. 2, ch. 2020-123. Registry has it (`fs-379-354`).
- Exemptions, § 379.353(2)(a) to (q): PRIMARY, flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/379.353, last amended ch. 2024-251. Registry note says the 65+ exemption was "not re-read": it is (2)(n), resident 65 or over, proof of age and residency; a no-cost licence from a tax collector "must be carried". (2)(l) exempts fishing from a licensed pier, which cannot reach a diver. No exemption fits a diver except (a) under 16, (c) military on leave, (i)/(j) licensed vessel, (n) 65+.
- Vessel licence, § 379.354(7)(f): PRIMARY. Individual scuba divers taking saltwater products from a vessel whose operator holds the vessel licence need no individual licence or permits. Registry marks this "secondary, confirm wording": now confirmed on flsenate.gov. Note the word is "scuba divers"; a freediver on a dive charter is not obviously covered. Conservative reading: freedivers carry their own licence.
- Shoreline licence: statute (4)(k) is silent on swimming/diving; FWC licence page (myfwc.com/license/recreational/saltwater-fishing/) says "Not valid if taking or attempting to take by swimming or diving." Registry already records this.
- Landing: FWC page: "A Florida fishing license is required to land saltwater species in Florida regardless of where they are caught." Registry effect line already says so.
- Lobster permit: FWC page: "Required, in addition to a saltwater fishing license, when taking or attempting to take lobster" ($5/yr). Not in regs.json licences list. The spear panel covers lobster only as "may not be speared"; the many divers who spear and catch lobster on the same dive need the permit. Statutory home not located this session (lead: § 379.354(4)(d)/(f) "crawfish permit").
- State Reef Fish Survey, r. 68B-14.009: PRIMARY flrules: history "New 8-26-14, Amended 6-11-18, 7-1-20, 9-1-26"; final notice ID 31244881 eff. 9/1/2026. Registry has it with the 1 Sep 2026 amendment. FWC licence page says "No exemptions" and ages "16 and older"; the rule's (3) exempts persons under § 379.353(2)(a), (i), (j), (o), i.e. under-16s, licensed-vessel customers and FWC staff. - HMS Angling permit, 50 C.F.R. § 635.4(c): PRIMARY eCFR (up to date 22 Sep 2026). "The owner of any vessel used to fish recreationally for Atlantic HMS" must hold one; a shark endorsement is additionally required to retain sharks; § 635.4(a)(10) says stricter state rules govern in state waters. Low priority: the rule that bites a spearo is § 635.21(h), already covered.
- Spearfishing endorsement: none exists in Florida. FWC's permit list (snook, lobster, tarpon tag, etc.) has no spearfishing permit. Confirmed negative worth a one-line row so users stop asking.
